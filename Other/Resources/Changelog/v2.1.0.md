# Release notes

## Changes

- Replaced the configuration-keys `prescript` and `postscript` in `Adame.configuration` by the 4 configuration-keys `prestartscript`, `poststartscript`, `prestopscript` and `poststopscript` to allow to hook into the container-start and the container-stop before and after the docker-compose-call.
- Existing repositories will be migrated automatically: The value of `prescript` will be moved to `prestartscript` and the value of `postscript` will be moved to `poststopscript`.
- Fixed that `Adame.configuration`, `Security.configuration`, the properties-file and the Adame-log-file were written with CRLF-line-endings on Windows. They are now always written with LF-line-endings.
